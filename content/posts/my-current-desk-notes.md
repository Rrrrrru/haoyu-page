I like a desk that disappears while I work. Fewer distractions, fewer cables in sight, and just enough tools to stay comfortable for a long stretch of writing or coding.

## What I keep nearby

- A mechanical keyboard I enjoy typing on
- A mouse that is comfortable for long sessions
- Headphones for blocking outside noise
- A notebook for quick offline thinking

## One habit that helps

I try to leave the desk ready for the next session. When I come back, I can start immediately instead of reorganizing everything first.

![Desk setup](/images/contents/youtube-my-desk-setup.jpg)

A clean setup does not make the work for me, but it removes enough friction that starting feels easy.
